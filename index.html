About Me

Hello! I'm Sharon McDermott, born and raised in Los Angeles—specifically, the intersection of Sepulveda and Venice, where the world began and ended outside a pink apartment building and its stubborn patch of grass.

I'm the youngest in my family, a position that came with a particular image: delicate, breakable, and needing protection. I didn't love that narrative, so I rewrote it. Tae Kwon Do became my plot twist—a way to show the world (and myself) that I could hold my own. Somewhere between sparring practice and broken boards, I discovered I liked surprising people—especially when that person was me.

Los Angeles raised me on art, cinema, and history. It's why I draw, can't resist a museum, and gravitate to films where nothing explodes, but everything matters. At ten, my world stretched 285 miles east to Las Vegas. The desert had fewer art-house theaters, but it was decorated in many personalities. I became an observer, a listener, a collector of stories that weren't mine but felt like they could be.

Psychology and philosophy slipped into my life like they'd always belonged there. Eventually, I pursued a B.S. in Psychology at UNLV, then became a research coordinator and realized I loved turning curiosity into data and data into meaning. That curiosity pushed me to earn an M.S. in Data Science—I didn't just want to ask questions; I wanted to find answers in the details.

Now, I'm drawn to a career where empathy meets inquiry—where storytelling meets structure.

Contact

Contact me if you want someone who can dive into the chaos of numbers and come back with a story that actually means something—that’s my sweet spot. I’ll bring the data, the curiosity, and the kind of clarity that makes the insight impossible to forget.
